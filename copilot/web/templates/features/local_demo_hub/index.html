For judges

Next step: {% if judge_as_buyer_enabled %}open the case as the sandbox buyer{% else %}open All disputes{% endif %}, then review the case as the seller. Nothing is sent to PayPal until you approve it.

Check your setup: run python tools/judge_check.py; it reads the local app and prints one line per step (see "Check your setup" in docs/JUDGE-RUN.md).

The setup script (tools/sandbox_seed.py) creates two sales by the same sandbox buyer in your own PayPal Sandbox account, one with a DEMO tracker; you open the dispute as that buyer in seven steps on the Judge as buyer page, and the inbox marks the seeded sale. Proofie does not sign in, open a dispute or send anything on the buyer's behalf.

At send, the hash of the approved package is written into the evidence line, and the dispute is read back from PayPal by GET before the send shows as confirmed.
